🎮 Updated Sonicity Game Loop (with your new ideas)
0. Pre-Season Setup (Global)

    Players stake NFTs to cities (collections).

    Players start earning Gold from active play.

    Each city has access to core "City Wide Buildings" list.

🏛 Cities start simple:

    Town Hall (basic)

    Treasury (closed)

    Barracks (closed)

    Diplomacy Center (closed)

    Bank (locked until built)

1. Early Week (Day 1-2): "Expansion Phase"

✅ Players use Gold to:

    Build Local Buildings:

        Homes (increase base Gold generation).

        Water Supply / Windmill / Factory (boost Home cap).

    Upgrade Town Hall:

        Unlocks ability to expand more Homes.

        Unlocks stronger city features (like bribe system access, higher LP contribution caps).

✅ Bribes start light:

    NFT creators or external players bribe for Temporary Buffs:

        Boost Gold production

        Boost LP rep points

        Temporary event defense

✅ Buffs are TEMPORARY (e.g., 2 days only) and must be bought via bribes.

    No permanent buffs from bribes — good balance ✅

2. Midweek (Day 3-5): "Specialization Phase"

✅ Cities unlock and start building City Wide Buildings:

    Treasury:

        Allows gold imports with heavy taxes.

        Limited amounts weekly.

    Barracks:

        Buy units for future arena battles (cool PvP later).

    Diplomacy Center:

        Start negotiations: alliances, trades, non-aggression.

    Bank:

        Needed for the season win (building the Bank is the new main goal).

✅ Bribery ramps up:

    Higher bribes from NFT creators for:

        Permanent upgrades (example: faster Barracks training speed)

        Temporary production boosts

        Immunity from disasters (short term)

✅ Gold inflation is kept under control:

    Only active players earn it (by building, staking, LP-ing).

    Sitting idle = no Gold farming.

    Possibly Gold decay if wallet inactive? (optional later)

3. Late Week (Day 5-7): "Victory Phase"

✅ Focus shifts to finishing the Bank:
Construction Type	Total Gold Needed	Purpose
Temporary Buff Bribes	100k Gold	Small gameplay boosts
Bank Construction (Win Condition)	500k Gold	Build the city's Bank and win

✅ Cities rush:

    Allocate maximum Gold to Bank Construction.

    Bribes fly around (maybe sabotage enemy bank building? Optional?).

✅ First city to complete the Bank wins the week/season!
4. Rewards and Post-Season

✅ Players in the winning city who:

    Staked NFTs actively

    Built Homes

    Provided LP

    Participated in Treasury/Barracks/Diplomacy

➡️ Receive Rewards:

    Gold token drops

    Relics (NFTs)

    Reputation points

    FeeM distributions

✅ Losing cities may still earn:

    Minor rewards

    Best individual players awards (top builder, top defender)

🧠 Things to Finalize Before Locking the System

    Gold Sitting Problem
    ✅ Solution: Gold production tied to actions (build → stake → LP → spend).
    ✅ Optional: Gold decay for inactive players (encourages active play).

    Temp vs Permanent Buffs
    ✅ Temp Buffs: Only through bribes (makes bribes important).
    ✅ Permanent Buffs: Only through Town Hall upgrades or City Buildings (must work for them).

    Bribery Economy
    ✅ Bribes = spenders give Gold to city Treasury (taxed at 10-30% burn maybe?) ✅ High bribes (100k+) can unlock strategic bonuses, not gamebreaking powers.

    LP and Rep Points
    ✅ LP providers earn Rep points (spent later on governance decisions or Diplomacy deals).

    Clear Distinction Between Buildings

Town Hall (Expansion Center)	Home (basic Gold generator)
Treasury (Gold imports/export)	Water Supply (home cap boost)
Barracks (unit training)	Windmill (home productivity boost)
