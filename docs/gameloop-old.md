# 🧙‍♂️ Sonicity Gameplay Loo 

Think about it as RTS not tied to crypto

## 🎈 1. Entry Phase: Start the Journey

- Player connects wallet.

- Player sees World Map (initially 3–5 cities).

- Player can  enter only to ine city. He can do so with whileisted NFT

- Player can only enter cities tied to their NFTs.

- Player chooses a city → enters city view.

## 🏩 2. Starter/District Buildings: Core Infrastructure

Every city starts with:

Building - Purpose

🔊 Altar - Stake NFTs → receive building slots / earn base Gold from Homes

⛏ Mine - Provide LP → earn Gold and Rep points (used in governance)

🏩 City Hall - Central hub: build and manage local upgrades + donate Gold to City Upgrades

## 🏡 3. Local Gameplay: Building Your District

- Player stakes NFT to get building slots for the District

- Each player manages their private economy:

Local Building - Purpose

🏠 Homes - Generate Gold income

🌾 Farms / Mills - Produce Food to sustain Homes

(Later expansions) - Factories, Windmills, Water Supply, etc.

🏩 City Hall - Build Dashboard, Stats, Buy upgrades, Fund Buildings

🔜 Home slots are limited — players must upgrade City Hall to grow but eventually hit a hard cap.

## 💰 4. City-Wide Gameplay: Treasury and Tiers

- City is a community owned space

- Players donate Gold at City Hall, which is locked to the City Treasury.

- When player donates gold re freceives Rep Points in return

- Tier 0 → Tier 1 is an introduction to city wide economics, where players learn to contribute together for the common good. After Tier 1 players can choose to what building contribute

- Each Tier increases building spots for all citizens

Treasury fills → unlocks City-Wide Tiers:

Treasury Tier - Buildings Unlocks

Tier 1 - Library (optimize food production, personal boosts), Marketplace (external Gold flow, daily limits, FIFO), Defense Tower

Tier 2 - Barracks (PvP unlocked), Church (donate Gold, receive temporal buffs), Mage Tower (stake relics), Lottery Hall

Tier 3 - Diplomacy Center (city governance), Bank (controlled Gold withdrawal system)

Tier 4+ - Access to Great Building projects

🔜 Each building is  upgradable.

🔜 Players shape city-wide upgrades through donations.

## ⚔️ 5. PvP Phase: Warfare Begins

- After Barracks (Tier 2) unlocks:

- Players can launch raids against rival cities.

Raid Success: Burn and Slot Redistribution

Result - Effect

Gold Burn - A percentage of the defeated city's Treasury Gold is burned (e.g., 5%)

Slot Rewards - Sponsors receive new Home slots (expand their local economy)

Slot Penalties - Defeated city's players lose Home slots with buildings on them (shrinks their economy)

- Raids require Gold sponsorship (players commit Gold before raid).

🔜 Cities have a Peace Shield until reaching Tier 1.

🔜 Raids cost Gold and involve real risk and strategy.

## 🛡 6. Expansion Phase: Growth Demands New Land

When players hit their Home caps:

Expand by:

- (Optional) Funding new district expansions via Treasury.

- Raiding/conquering enemy city districts.

🔜 World map evolves organically through conquest and settlement.

## 🏩 7. Great Buildings Phase: Race for World Domination

After reaching Tier 4:

Cities can start Great Building Projects:

- Golden Cathedral

- Arcane Tower

- Eternal Market

Great Buildings:

- Reshape Treasury flows (distribute treasury)

- Buff relic drop rates

- Enhance PvP strength

Cities have to actively use their treasury to defend, attack, govern and distribute.

🔜 Massive city efforts over weeks.

🔜 Cities must defend Great Buildings from raids.

🔜 Wonders become symbols of dominance.

## ⚔️ 8. Conflict Phase: Reclaim, Destroy, Expand

- Great Buildings can be captured.

- Cities can form coalition through governance

🔜 World stays dynamic and player-driven.

🔜 (Optional) expansion to new lands

## ⏳ 9. Season Soft Resets: Renew the World

Every 4–6 months:

- Ownership of Great Buildings resets.

- Some land ownership reshuffles.

Players keep:

- Relics

- City upgrades

- Rep points

🔜 Old players retain progress.

🔜 New players have fresh opportunities.

## 🎲 10. Random Events: Life Happens

Dynamic unpredictable events include:

- Fires, events, famines, bandit raids...

- Economic booms, festivals, miracle harvests...

🔜 Forces constant Gold movement.

🔜 Keeps gameplay unpredictable and lively.

## 💚 11. Relics: Eternal Power

Relics are special NFTs.

Obtained via:

- Relic Lotteries

- PvP Raids

- Special Events

Players must stake relics into the Mage Tower to activate buffs.

Staked relics:

- Provide player and/or city buffs (depends on rarity)

- Can be stolen during successful raids on the Mage Tower.

- Relics can be traded, transferred, or sold when unstaked.



🌟 Ultra-Simplified Gameplay Flow

Connect Wallet → Enter City
    ↓
Stake NFTs + Provide LP → Earn Gold
    ↓
Build Homes → Feed Homes → Upgrade City Hall
    ↓
Donate to City Treasury → Unlock Tiers (City Upgrades)
    ↓
Unlock PvP → Sponsor Raids → Raid and Defend
    ↓
Raid Success → Burn Enemy Gold → Gain Home Slots → Defeat Weakens Enemies
    ↓
Expand Districts → Race for Great Buildings
    ↓
Season Reset → Keep Relics and Prestige → New World Cycle
