Relic Categories & Examples
🪙 Economic Relics
Name	Effect
Coin of Abundance	+10% Gold production from all Houses
Seed of Seasons	-20% Food replenish cooldown
Crystal Echo	+1 Crystal per claim from Sanctum (max once/day)
Guild Ledger	+5% efficiency on all grid building conversions
Timeworn Bell	Reduces processing time by 10% for all Essence Forges
⚔️ Military / PvP Relics
Name	Effect
Banner of Resolve	+10% defense in PvP if city is under attack
Talisman of Formation	Allows 1 extra troop slot in formation
Ashen Horn	Reduces cooldown between PvP actions by 4h
Ember Gauntlet	+10% attack bonus for first round of any PvP battle
Scout’s Compass	Opponent search reveals formation history
📜 Governance & Political Relics
Name	Effect
Seal of Voices	+10% vote weight for governance NFT
Scroll of Memory	Allows re-vote on a failed proposal (1/week)
Torch of Unity	Doubles council reward for one successful vote (cooldown)
Tome of Titles	Allows re-naming your city once
Oathstone Fragment	Used to form permanent alliances (burn after use)
🧭 Exploration / PvE Relics
Name	Effect
Lantern of Fog	Reveals 2 extra tiles per move
Relic Map	1-time use — sends hero to hidden treasure site
Tethered Grimoire	Allows one-time reroll of PvE battle result
Key of Seasons	Unlocks seasonal reward dungeon
Breath of Wind	Hero moves twice in one day (once/week)
