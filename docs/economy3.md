    Would creators/players want to buy Gold from the open market to use it for bribes?

    Yes... but only if bribes really feel like powerful strategic accelerators.

    If bribes change the city’s destiny (even slightly),
    then NFT creators and big players will have a real reason to buy Gold and push their city over the line.

    How to Make Buy Pressure Meaningful But Safe

✅ Bribes Should Feel Important But Not Overpowered

    Bribes should only boost city production or defense, NOT directly build the Bank.

    Example:

        "Bribe success = +20% faster Home construction for 48 hours."

        Not "bribe success = instantly finish Bank stage."

✅ Tax Open Market Gold Conversion Heavily

    Buying Gold on open market → using it for bribes → 30%-50% Gold tax when spending bought Gold.

    Native earned Gold from city has no tax.

✅ Limited Weekly Bribe Slots

    Each city can only accept a certain amount of bribe Gold per week.

    Players must bid or fight to be the top briber.

✅ Special Events Needing Open Market Gold

    Maybe some city disasters (or late-game rushes) are best solved by "emergency bribes".

    Example: "Golden Famine incoming! Only players with emergency Gold bribes can save food supply."

✅ Artifact Auctions Paid Only in Open Gold

    Occasionally auction special Relics/Artifacts only in Market Gold.

    This creates massive gold-buying spikes mid-season.
