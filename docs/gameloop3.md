🧩 Sonicity Gameplay Loop (Finalized Structure — April 2025)
🛫 1. Entry Phase: Start the Journey

    Player connects wallet.

    Player sees the World Map (initially 3–5 cities).

    Player can only enter cities tied to their NFTs.

    Player chooses a city → enters city view.

✅ Immediate connection between NFT ownership and active gameplay.
🏛 2. Starter Buildings: Core Infrastructure

At the start, every city provides:
Building	Purpose
🕊 Altar	Stake NFTs → earn base Gold
⛏ Mine	Provide LP → earn Gold and reputation
🏛 City Hall	Central hub: manage local upgrades + donate Gold to City Treasury

✅ From day one, players have access to Gold generation and city evolution mechanics.
🏡 3. Local Gameplay: Building Your Domain

Inside the city, each player manages their private economy:
Local Building	Purpose
🏠 Homes	Generate Gold income (requires Food)
🌾 Farms / Mills	Produce Food to sustain Homes
Possibly other buildings later like factories, windmills, water supply, etc.
🏛 City Hall (local side)	Expand Home slots, reduce upkeep costs, unlock personal boosts

✅ Home slots are limited — players must expand strategically.

✅ Upgrading City Hall locally allows more Homes but only to a hard limit.

✅ Players are forced toward city expansion or conquest once they max out their private land.
💰 4. City-Wide Gameplay: Treasury and Tiers

    Players donate Gold to the City Treasury via City Hall.

    Treasury fills → Tiers unlock, bringing massive city upgrades:

Treasury Tier	Unlocks
Tier 1	Library (allows local upgrades, like optimize food supply), Marketplace (to allow exteranl flow, has daily limits, fifo), Defense Tower
Tier 2	Barracks (PvP unlocks), Diplomacy Center (for city governance), Church (city buffs)
Tier 3	Bank (controlled Gold withdrawal system, has daily limits, fifo)
Tier 4+	Access to Great Building projects

✅ Collective donations shape city evolution.

✅ Players feel personal responsibility for city success.
⚔️ 5. PvP Phase: Warfare Begins

After Barracks (Tier 2) is built:

    Players can raid rival cities:

        Burn enemy Treasury Gold

        Damage enemy districts

    Raids cost Gold and risk failure.

✅ New cities are protected by a Peace Shield until reaching Tier 1.

✅ PvP creates tension, risk, and real Gold movement in the system.
🛡 6. Expansion Phase: Growth Demands New Land

When players hit Home caps:

    They must expand by:

        Funding district expansions via massive city Treasury projects.

        OR raiding and conquering enemy city districts.

✅ Expansion becomes a giant Gold sink.

✅ New lands = new Home slots, new vulnerabilities, new ambitions.
🏛 7. Great Buildings Phase: World Domination Race

After reaching Tier 4:

    Cities can start Great Building projects:

        Golden Cathedral

        Arcane Tower

        Eternal Market

    Great Buildings:

        Tax other cities

        Buff relic generation

        Strengthen PvP dominance

✅ Building wonders takes weeks of Gold contribution.

✅ Cities must defend their projects from enemy sabotage.

✅ Wonders become world-defining monuments.
⚔️ 8. Conflict Phase: Reclaim, Destroy, Expand

    Great Buildings can be raided or captured by rivals.

    Districts can be conquered through coordinated PvP.

    Alliances and betrayals naturally emerge.

✅ True dynamic RTS-level warfare, not static farming.

✅ Glory, betrayal, domination — all real player stories.
⏳ 9. Season Soft Resets: Renew the World

Every 4–6 months:

    Ownership of Great Buildings resets.

    District ownership partially reshuffles.

    Players retain:

        Relics

        Core city upgrades

        Fame, titles, prestige

✅ Progress stays meaningful.
✅ New players can join without feeling hopeless.

✅ World narrative keeps evolving every season.
🎲 10. Random Events: Life Happens

World stays alive with unpredictable events:

    Fires, plagues, famines, bandit attacks...

    Economic booms, city festivals, miracle harvests...

✅ Forces constant Gold usage.
✅ Randomness breaks routines, keeps players reacting.
🎯 Ultra-Simplified Gameplay Flow

Connect Wallet → Enter City
    ↓
Stake NFTs + Provide LP → Earn Gold
    ↓
Build Homes → Feed Homes → Upgrade City Hall (Personal)
    ↓
Donate to City Treasury → Unlock Tiers (City-Wide Upgrades)
    ↓
Unlock PvP → Raid and Defend → Expand Districts
    ↓
Race for Great Buildings → Dominate World
    ↓
Season Reset → Keep Relics and Glory → Begin New Era

🧠 Design Principles Locked In:

✅ Gold always needed — for upkeep, upgrades, war, expansion.
✅ Players can't sit idle — must act, build, fight, cooperate.
✅ Cities live and die — real growth, conquest, and resets.
✅ Player emotional investment in both personal domain and city survival.

✅ Sonicity = medieval RTS x decentralized economy.
