🏗️ 1. "Player Journey" (First Experience)

✅ Connect Wallet

    "Welcome, City Founder! Choose your NFT collection to represent."

✅ Receive Starter Gold

    Each participant gets a small amount of "Gold" to start building immediately.

✅ Choose Your First Structure

        Mine (produce more Gold)

        Bank (boost LP token rewards)

        Barracks (future bribe/defense stuff)

✅ Stake NFTs

    Stake NFTs from your collection to increase your weekly influence (affects voting/bribes/loot share).

✅ Watch Your City Grow

    See a simple visual (map, list, or 2D mini city) that updates with each new building.

✅ End of Week — Battle for Rewards

        Top collections (by influence points) win a share of fees, token emissions, bribes.

        Players who staked their NFTs can claim rewards after results!

🏦 2. "Game Economy v1" (Simple but Alive)
Resource	How You Get It	What You Do With It
Gold	Starter pack, plus passive production	Build structures, contribute to LP pools
Influence Points	Building + staking actions	Compete for weekly wins
Bribe Tokens	(Optional) Pay your way to attract players to your collection	Boost your influence temporarily
Weekly Rewards	Claimable if your collection wins	Gold, fees, governance power, cosmetic boosts
