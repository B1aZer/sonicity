# 🏛 Sonicity: NFT City Wars

**Build, Bribe, Conquer**

## 📜 Game Overview

**Sonicity** is a strategic city-building competition where NFT collections battle to create the most prosperous and powerful cities.  
Players **build structures**, **provide liquidity (LP)**, **accept bribes**, and **manage their economy** to earn tokens, rewards, and fame.

Every week, the collection that generates the most **Influence** wins the season — unlocking staking rewards for its NFT holders and sharing fees, token emissions, and bribe earnings.

## 🧩 Core Mechanics

### 1. Starting Phase: City Founding

- Every participating NFT collection player starts with a **minimal Gold income** for staking NFTs from the whitelisted collections.
- **Gold** can be used to build key structures: homes, banks, bribe markets, LP stations, and more.
- NFT holders cooperate to strengthen their city's economy, defenses, and strategy.

### 2. Building Structures

Players spend Gold to construct and upgrade buildings that:

- Produce more Gold
- Unlock LP bonuses
- Amplify bribe power
- Strengthen city defenses
- Unlock unique city buffs

*Strategic decisions about what to build and when are key to dominance.*

### 3. Providing Liquidity (LP Stations)

- Certain buildings allow players to **provide liquidity** for the in-game **Gold token**.
- LP providers earn **Reputation Points** — a valuable in-game currency that can only be earned by providing liquidity.

*Underneath, a real DeFi LP pool operates, directly connecting city actions to actual tokenomics.*

### 4. Bribes & Diplomacy

NFT collection creators and external players can **offer bribes** to influence the outcome of the season.

Bribes can:

- Speed up building times
- Boost LP rewards
- Strengthen city-wide bonuses
- Sabotage or weaken rival cities

*Bribes can be public or secret — strategic use will often decide the fate of a city.*

### 5. Weekly Competitions

Every week, the NFT collection with the highest **Influence Score** (based on building progress, LP provided, and bribes received) wins the season.

## 🎁 Rewards & Staking

### Winner's Rewards

The winning collection shares:

- Fee revenue from the LP pools
- Allocated Gold token emissions
- All collected bribe amounts
- Special artifacts, relics, and FeeM boosts

### NFT Staking

After victory, holders of the winning collection's NFTs who actively participated in city-building can **stake their NFTs** to receive ongoing weekly rewards:

- Gold tokens
- Fee/FeeM revenue
- Special artifacts or relic NFTs
- Governance rights for future seasons

*Longer staking = greater rewards. Legendary or rare NFTs can unlock boosted staking yields.*

## 🎲 Special Systems and Features

### Wonders and Legendary Buildings

- Unique **one-per-season** structures offering massive bonuses.
- First city to build them gains long-term advantages.

### Artifacts and Relics

- Special in-game NFTs found or earned during play.
- Can be **traded**, **kept**, or **sacrificed** for massive buffs to the city's strength or economy.

---

## 🛡 Diplomacy and Betrayal

- Collections can **form alliances**, **trade bribes**, or **betray each other** for advantage.
- Political gameplay is just as important as economic gameplay.

---

## ⚡ City Disasters and Random Events

- Randomized events like **economic booms**, **natural disasters**, **plagues**, or **bandit raids** may occur mid-season.
- Cities must adapt quickly or risk losing their lead.

---

## 🏗 Game Loop (Simplified)

- **Week Starts** → All cities receive Gold from staked NFTs → Build structures, LP, and strategize bribes.
- **Midweek Events** → Random events (good or bad) impact city strategies.
- **Week Ends** → Influence Scores are tallied → Winning collection is declared.
- **NFT Staking Opens** → Winning collection's holders stake NFTs for rewards.
- **New Week Starts** → Fresh Gold, new bribes, new battles begin.
