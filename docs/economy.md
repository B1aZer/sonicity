🏛 Real Example:

    A player wants to help their city build a Wonder (e.g., The Great Bank of Atlantis).

    It costs 100,000 Gold across all players.

    Players who don't have enough Gold buy it from the market to rush the build before other cities.

    ➡ Creates insane mid/late-season market demand.

💣 Even better:

You can design Gold to be burned when used for:

    Crafting

    Entering events

    Building ultimate structures

    Unlocking special buffs

✨ Burn = Permanent Supply Reduction
✨ More burn = More scarcity = More value
🧠 Big Secret

In every strong game economy (even old ones like WoW, EVE Online, RuneScape),
there are ALWAYS "luxury sinks":

    Items so desirable players buy/craft/burn gold to get them.

    Not everyone needs them — but enough players want them to drive demand.

You should design Gold sinks that create aspiration.
