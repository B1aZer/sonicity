⚠️ Do NOT fund rewards from recharge back to same player
That’s circular and kills token utility.

Here’s a better loop:
Step	Description
Player recharges buildings → Sonic → Treasury	
Treasury grows = City fund	
Player reaches T4, mints Dynamic NFT	
Player stakes NFT into governance	
Governance decides what % of Treasury to distribute	
Rewards shared proportionally to NFT weight (Rep amount, hold time)	
→ This creates real yield from activity without a feedback loop.
