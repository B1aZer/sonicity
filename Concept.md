# 🏛 NFT City Wars: Build, Bribe, Conquer
## 📜 Game Overview

NFT City Wars is a strategic city-building game where different NFT collections compete to create the most prosperous and powerful cities.
Players build structures, provide liquidity (LP), accept bribes, and manage their city's economy to earn tokens, rewards, and fame.

Every week, the collection that generates the most influence wins the season — unlocking staking rewards for its NFT holders and sharing fees, token emissions, and bribe earnings.

## 🧩 Core Mechanics

1. Starting Phase: City Founding

- Every participating NFT collection player starts with a minimal amount of Gold.
- Gold can be used to build city structures: farms, banks, bribe markets, LP stations, and more.
- NFT holders cooperate to strengthen their city's economy and defenses.

2. Building Structures

Players spend Gold to construct and upgrade buildings that:

- Produce more Gold
- Unlock LP bonuses
- Enhance bribe power
- Strengthen city defenses

Strategic decisions about what to build and when are key to success.

3. Providing Liquidity (LP Stations)

- Certain buildings allow players to provide liquidity for the in-game Gold token.
- LP providers earn influence points, Gold boosts, and bonus rewards.

Underneath, a real DeFi LP pool is operating, connecting game actions to actual tokenomics.

4. Bribes & Diplomacy

NFT collection leaders and external players can offer bribes to influence outcomes.

Bribes can:

- Speed up building
- Boost LP rewards
- Strengthen city bonuses
- Weaken rival cities

Bribes are public or secret, and players must strategize how to use them.

5. Weekly Competitions

Every week, the collection with the highest Influence Score (based on building progress, LP provided, bribes received) wins the season.

## 🎁 Rewards & Staking
Winner's Rewards:

The winning collection shares:
- Fee revenue from the LP pools
- Gold token allocation
- All collected bribe amounts
- FeeM

NFT Staking:

After victory, holders of the winning collection's NFTs can stake their NFTs for ongoing weekly rewards:

- Gold tokens
- Special artifacts or relic NFTs
- Governance rights for future seasons
- Longer staking = greater rewards.

Legendary/rare NFTs might have boosted staking yields.

## 🎲 Special Systems and Features
Wonders and Legendary Buildings

Unique one-per-season structures that offer huge bonuses to the city that builds them first.

Artifacts and Relics

Special in-game NFTs found or earned that boost city performance.

Can be traded, kept, or sacrificed for massive buffs.

## Diplomacy and Betrayal

    Collections can form alliances, trade bribes, or betray each other for advantage.

## City Disasters and Random Events

    Random events like economic booms, natural disasters, or bandit attacks can change the city's fate mid-season.

## 🏗 Game Loop (Simplified)

Week Starts → All cities get Gold, build, LP, accept bribes.
Midweek Events → Random bonuses or disasters occur.
Week Ends → Influence Scores tallied → Winner declared.
NFT Staking Opens → Winning collection's holders stake for rewards.
Next Week Starts → New gold, new bribes, new battles.
